Email ian@devworksllc.com for anything — a bug, a question about your account, a state form that needs fixing, or feedback. We read every message and usually reply within a couple of days.

Common questions

How do I change how many hours my state requires?

State requirements are built in and update automatically — there's nothing to configure. If a number looks wrong, email us the state and we'll check it against the DMV's current rule.

Why isn't my state's official form available to export?

We've added official pre-filled forms for 29 states so far, with more coming. In the meantime, every state can still export a signed affidavit and the full drive log as a PDF, which is accepted by most DMVs.

How do I add a second student, or share with my co-parent?

That's part of Family Pack, a one-time $9.99 purchase (not a subscription) in the app's Account screen. It removes the one-student limit and lets you share a dashboard with another parent or supervisor.

How do I delete my data?

See our data deletion page — you can delete a single student or your whole account directly in the app, no email required.

Does the app track my location when I'm not driving?

No. Location is only ever recorded while a drive timer is actively running. See our Privacy Policy for the full details.
